Research directions this work is aligned with
  • Comparative research linking Malaysia and Finland on reflective cognition, digital ecosystems, and parental wellbeing.
  • Evidence-based insights that inform national policy and international discussions on AI in education.
  • Inclusive, human-centered approaches to digital transformation in parental education.

Dr. Elaine’s research on parental education and the 5Q MindVehicle™ Framework is inspired by and developed in alignment with the UNESCO Chair’s focus on AI-supported platforms in education. This shared direction informs how her work approaches digital innovation and community impact in Malaysia and beyond.
